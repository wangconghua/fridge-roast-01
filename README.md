## Why this line

The judge brief ranks its criteria most important first: the line must be a
roast of a smart fridge, the punchline must land, the joke must be specific
to smart fridges, the wording must be tight, and the angle must be fresh —
explicitly dodging the obvious cracks about the fridge ordering groceries or
watching you. This line does all five: it roasts the ice maker by name, it is
a gag that only works on a fridge with an ice maker, the punch ("limited
edition") turns a broken appliance into a marketing joke in one beat, and it
contains neither of the worn moves. It reads cleanly in one breath.
